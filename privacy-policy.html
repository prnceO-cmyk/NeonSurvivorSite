Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

Neon Survivor is built to be a simple, fast, and ad-free experience. We do not run advertising networks, third-party analytics, or data collection scripts in the game or on this website.

2. What we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information such as:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your gameplay history or usage analytics.
  • Advertising IDs, device identifiers, or cross-app tracking data.
  • Any data for ad targeting or marketing purposes.

3. Local game data

Any save files or configuration created locally on your device remain under your control and are stored only on your machine unless you choose to share them. This game does not depend on a remote account system.

4. Website access

This website is intentionally lightweight and does not include advertising pixels, tracking scripts, or external analytics services. We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling.

5. Third-party services

We do not use third-party ad networks, remarketing platforms, or data brokers. If we add optional cloud services in the future, we will update this policy clearly and transparently before enabling them.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the project maintainer through the channels listed on the game website or project repository.

No ads. No trackers. No data selling.
